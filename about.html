ABOUT ME / 01

Designing with intention, creating with curiosity.

Hi, I'm Mai, a graphic designer based in Toronto, with a passion for branding, packaging, editorial design, and motion. This is a little more about my journey, my approach, and what inspires me.

01

MY STORY

From strategy to visual storytelling.

Before studying graphic design, I built a foundation in marketing, which shaped how I think about audiences, communication, and the purpose behind a creative idea. My journey into design began with a curiosity for how visual elements can turn ideas into meaningful experiences.

02

MY APPROACH

Clarity with character.

I believe good design starts with understanding the problem, not just finding a visual solution. My process balances research, strategy, and creativity — from initial exploration to refining the details. I'm drawn to work that feels both purposeful and visually engaging.

03

BEYOND DESIGN

A few things that inspire me.

Outside of design, I'm inspired by photography, architecture, books, and everyday moments. These interests help me see the world with a fresh perspective and bring new ideas into my work.
