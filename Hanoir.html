BRANDING & VISUAL IDENTITY

Hanoir —Modern Heritage.

Exploring the relationship between Vietnamese cultural identity and contemporary fashion through a thoughtful visual language.

PROJECT TYPE

Brand Identity

MY ROLE

Research, Concept & Design

YEAR

2026

TOOLS

Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

01/

THE CHALLENGE

Every design begins with a question.

How can a contemporary fashion brand express the cultural character of Hanoi without relying on predictable traditional imagery?

Hanoir explores this question by bringing together the city's layered history, everyday poetry, and contemporary way of life. The goal was to develop an identity that feels connected to its roots while remaining relevant to a modern audience.

02/

RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

Looking beyond the obvious.

Before developing the visual identity, I explored the cultural references, architectural details, textiles, colours, and everyday scenes that make Hanoi distinctive.

Rather than treating heritage as a decorative element, I wanted to understand which qualities could inform the brand's tone, materials, and visual language.

KEY INSIGHT

Heritage does not have to be recreated literally. It can be communicated through atmosphere, material, proportion, and detail.

03/

CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

From research to a visual idea.

I translated my research into the concept "Modern Heritage." This direction became the foundation for the brand's typography, colour palette, composition, and image-making.

Moodboard — exploring colour, typography, fabric, and visual atmosphere.

04/

TESTING & REFINEMENT

Good ideas need room to evolve.

I tested different visual directions before refining the final system. Comparing variations helped me evaluate how well each choice communicated the intended personality of Hanoir.

What I explored

Different typography pairings, colour relationships, compositions, and ways to apply the identity across materials.

What I refined

Visual consistency, readability, hierarchy, and the balance between cultural references and contemporary expression.

05/

THE FINAL DESIGN

A visual identity brought to life.

The final direction brings the concept together through a consistent system of typography, colour, imagery, and material choices. Each application is designed to feel like part of the same brand story.

06 / REFLECTION

Design is not just what people see.
It is what every decision communicates.

This project reminded me that visual design becomes more meaningful when every choice has a reason behind it. Research gives a concept depth, experimentation reveals new possibilities, and refinement helps bring the idea into focus.

My approach is to stay curious, question the obvious solution, and build visual experiences that balance clarity, intention, and character.
