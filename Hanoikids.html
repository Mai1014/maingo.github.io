BRANDING & CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Hanoikids Recruitment Campaign

A recruitment campaign for Hanoikids, a student-led organization offering free English tours with local students in Hanoi. The visual materials incorporate local cultural elements to reflect the organization’s identity and engage prospective members.

PROJECT TYPE

Campaign Design

MY ROLE

Campaign Visual, Event Materials Design,
Concept & Design

YEAR

2023

TOOLS

Illustrator, Photoshop

01/

RESEARCH & CONTEXT

What makes a recruitment campaign memorable?

One of our biggest challenges was building brand awareness among university students across Hanoi. As an independent organization without institutional support, we relied on sponsorships and contributions from tourists to fund our activities. We also faced competition from university clubs and other organizations with stronger networks and resources.

To stand out, the campaign used Hanoikids’ free English tours as a central design element, celebrating Hanoi’s local culture and the meaningful connections between local students and international visitors. By combining familiar Hanoi-inspired visuals with the spirit of youth, friendship, and cultural exchange, the campaign aimed to communicate the organization’s unique identity and encourage students to become part of the Hanoikids community and make it a memorable part of their university years.

02/

VISUAL EXPLORATION

Inspiration all around us.

Inspired by Hanoi’s local culture and the spirit of youth, the campaign incorporated familiar tour destinations as key design elements, celebrating the city’s charm while reflecting Hanoikids’ values of friendship, cultural exchange, and shared experiences.

Beyond famous tourist spots, we brought in little things that Hanoi students see and experience every day. From familiar streets to local landmarks and everyday city life, these elements helped the campaign feel closer to home, bringing out students’ love for Hanoi and encouraging them to be part of the Hanoikids community.

KEY INSIGHT

Students don’t just want to explore Hanoi.
They want to feel connected to the city they call home.

03/

CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

From research to a visual idea.

Why Binoculars?
Binoculars are all about curiosity and discovering something new. We chose them to show how Hanoikids helps students see their hometown from a different perspective, beyond the places they pass every day. They represent our love for exploring Hanoi, sharing its stories with international visitors, and making new memories together.

Moodboard — exploring colour, typography, fabric, and visual atmosphere.

04/

DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

Good ideas need room to evolve.

I tested different visual directions before refining the final system. Comparing variations helped me evaluate how well each choice communicated the intended personality of Hanoir.

What I explored

Different typography pairings, colour relationships, compositions, and ways to apply the identity across materials.

What I refined

Visual consistency, readability, hierarchy, and the balance between cultural references and contemporary expression.

05/

THE FINAL DESIGN

A visual identity brought to life.

The final direction brings the concept together through a consistent system of typography, colour, imagery, and material choices. Each application is designed to feel like part of the same brand story.

06 / REFLECTION

Design is not just what people see.
It is what every decision communicates.

This project reminded me that visual design becomes more meaningful when every choice has a reason behind it. Research gives a concept depth, experimentation reveals new possibilities, and refinement helps bring the idea into focus.

My approach is to stay curious, question the obvious solution, and build visual experiences that balance clarity, intention, and character.
